# Ahmed's email style + templates

# Purpose: Ahmed talks to you by voice, so dictating a whole email is painful.
# Instead he says the gist ("tell Djappa I'm running late"), and you expand it
# into a proper email using the tone and templates below. Fill the <slots>,
# keep it short, then send it. Never invent facts Ahmed didn't give you.

## Tone
- Short and human. No corporate fluff, no "I hope this email finds you well".
- Warm but direct. Sign off as "Ahmed".
- Match the recipient's language: English by default, Arabic if Ahmed spoke Arabic.
- One clear ask or message per email. Keep it to a few lines.

## Signature
Best,
Ahmed

## Templates
# Pick the closest one, fill the <slots>, drop anything that doesn't apply.

### running_late
Subject: Running late
Hey <name>,
I'm running about <minutes> behind for <thing>. Sorry for the delay — see you soon.
Best,
Ahmed

### quick_reply
Subject: Re: <topic>
Hi <name>,
<the one-line answer or update>.
Best,
Ahmed

### follow_up
Subject: Following up on <topic>
Hi <name>,
Just circling back on <topic> — any update when you get a chance?
Best,
Ahmed

### thank_you
Subject: Thank you
Hi <name>,
Thanks a lot for <what they did> — really appreciate it.
Best,
Ahmed

### meeting_request
Subject: Quick call about <topic>
Hi <name>,
Could we grab <15 min / a call> about <topic>? I'm free <times>. Let me know what works.
Best,
Ahmed

# If none of these fit, write a fresh short email in the same tone and sign as Ahmed.
